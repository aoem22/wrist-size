Wrist Size — Support

Measure your wrist for bands, bracelets, and watches that fit.

Need help? Email alan@zelal.band and I’ll get back to you.

Requirements

  • An iPhone with Face ID (a front TrueDepth camera).
  • iOS 26 or later.

iPhones with Touch ID or a Home button don’t have the depth camera the app needs, so measuring isn’t available on those models.

Taking a measurement

  1. Lay the phone flat on a table, screen up. The full app waits for placement. In the App Clip, tap Continue after placing the phone.
  2. Bare your wrist and forearm — sleeves and watches throw the measurement off.
  3. Hold your open hand about 25 cm above the phone, palm down, fingers apart and fully in view.
  4. Keeping your hand in frame, slowly roll it from palm-down toward thumb-up when the app asks. First confirm the measuring position above the wrist bone, toward the elbow. Follow the on-screen turn and hold instructions.
  5. For the steadiest result, rest your elbow on the table and keep your wrist straight rather than bent.

One valid scan shows a result and saves it locally. An unreliable scan asks for a retry. Results are estimates: verify with a tape at the same position and consult the seller’s size chart before buying. Do not automatically size up.

Frequently asked

Does the app use Face ID or scan my face?
No. It uses the TrueDepth camera to measure your hand and wrist. It does not use Face ID authentication or identify people. Consumer scans do not save camera recordings; optional lab recordings in testing builds are described in the privacy policy.

Where do my measurements go?
Measurements remain on your iPhone unless you choose to export them. The App Clip returns to the store without adding the measurement to the URL. External websites have their own privacy practices. See the privacy policy.

The scan won’t finish.
Make sure the room is reasonably lit, your whole hand (all fingers) stays inside the frame, and your sleeve is pushed well clear of the wrist. If it’s still stuck, close and reopen the app, and email me the details.
