Privacy Policy

Wrist Size and Zelal Fit App Clip — updated September 26, 2026

Measurement processing happens on your iPhone. The app and App Clip have no accounts, advertising, analytics, or automatic measurement uploads.

Camera and depth data

The front TrueDepth camera provides live images and depth data to estimate wrist circumference. Processing and hand tracking happen on the device. Consumer scans do not save photos, videos, or raw depth recordings. The app does not use Face ID authentication or identify people.

Local storage

The app keeps measurements, optional profile names, preferences, and technical scan summaries in its own storage. App Clip storage is temporary and can be removed by iOS; do not rely on it as a permanent history. iOS backup and migration from the App Clip to the full app is governed by your device and Apple settings. Removing the full app removes its local container, but does not erase exports or existing device backups.

Testing and diagnostic recordings

Development and TestFlight builds include a Wrist Lab for measurement validation. A tester can explicitly enable raw depth recording in the lab; it is off by default for lab scans and is not enabled by the consumer flow. Diagnostic recordings may contain camera or depth information about whatever is visible to the front camera. They stay on the device unless the tester chooses to export them. App Store builds do not expose the Wrist Lab.

Sharing

You may choose to export measurement history or, in testing builds, diagnostics using the system share sheet. Data is sent only to the destination you choose. Consider the contents before sharing. The app does not automatically send exports to us.

Store and other website links

The Zelal Fit App Clip receives the product URL used to launch it. The return link keeps the product and a numeric Shopify variant identifier; it does not add your wrist measurement, profile, or size to the URL. Opening the store, support page, privacy policy, or other external links opens a website whose privacy practices apply separately. Websites may receive normal request information such as your IP address.

Apple services and on-device libraries

Hand tracking uses a bundled on-device model. The app has no analytics or advertising SDK. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, and crash information according to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy practices; these services are separate from the app's measurement processing.

Contact

For privacy questions, email alan@zelal.band. Information you choose to include in an email is received by the support recipient and email providers.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes.
