Privacy Policy

Wrist Size — effective July 23, 2026

The short version: Wrist Size collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and the app makes no internet connections. Everything the app does happens on your device and stays there.

Camera and depth data

Wrist Size uses the front depth (TrueDepth) camera to measure your wrist. Camera frames and depth data are processed live, on your device, only while you are scanning. They are used to compute a measurement and are then discarded — the app does not save photos, videos, or depth recordings, and nothing from the camera ever leaves your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Measurements — your wrist measurement history, kept locally so you can look sizes up later.
  • People — optional names you add to keep measurements for different people (e.g. family members) apart. These stay on your device.
  • Settings — small preferences such as whether you have seen the intro.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Sharing and export

You can export your measurement history from the app. An export happens only when you start it, and the data goes only to the destination you choose in the share sheet. The app never sends anything on its own.

No tracking, no third parties

The app contains no analytics or advertising frameworks and does not connect to the internet. Hand tracking runs on an on-device machine-learning model; no third party receives any data. The App Store privacy label for Wrist Size is “Data Not Collected.”

Children

Wrist Size does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated here before that version ships, with a new effective date.
